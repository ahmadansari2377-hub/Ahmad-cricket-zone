How to play

Webcam controls

  • Swing your arms like a real bat. Timing decides the shot
  • Swing harder for more power, gently to block
  • Swing across your body for leg side, straighter for off side
  • Swing upwards to loft it over the field
  • Move your head to lean and duck in POV

Keyboard

  • Batting: Mouse aim · Space swing · hold Shift loft · S block
  • Bowling: Mouse place the target on the pitch · 1–4 variation · Space/Click bowl
  • C POV / TV camera   Tab scorecard   P pause

Your camera picture is processed on this device and never uploaded or saved. The tracking library (Google MediaPipe) sends anonymous usage statistics, never images.
